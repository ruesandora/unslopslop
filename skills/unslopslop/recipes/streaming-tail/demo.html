Streaming tail

When text arrives in pieces, the newest words show in a warm tone and settle into the ink colour over about 600 ms. The growing edge is easy to follow and the finished text is plain, with no blinking cursor and no fading of what is already there.

How it is built
Each word is a span stamped with its arrival time. One loop sets each young word's colour from its age, mixing from the tail colour to the ink colour with an ease-out curve; words older than the settle time are left as plain text. Nothing moves, so layout never shifts.
Colour
The tail colour must still pass contrast on its own (here about 4.8:1), because a reader may stop on the newest word. Pick it from the product's accent, not a rainbow.
Reduced motion
Words appear in ink with no colour change.
Becomes slop when
Finished text keeps shimmering, each word flies in with a blur, or the tail colour is so pale the newest words cannot be read.
