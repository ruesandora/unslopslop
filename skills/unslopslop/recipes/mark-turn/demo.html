Mark with a hidden turn

A small surprise for people who touch the logo: the mark changes costume for a season or a launch, piece by piece. A hat drops in from above and overshoots, a pompom pops a beat later, cheeks and a scarf follow, the eyes squint, and a snowflake flickers like a candle. The resting mark never changes, so the brand stays the brand.

Timing
Body colour 300 ms. Hat 460 ms cubic-bezier(.34, 1.56, .5, 1) from the hat brim; pompom 400 ms with 120 ms delay; scarf 380 ms from the left edge with 50 ms delay; cheeks 360 ms with 80 ms delay; eyes 320 ms with overshoot. Twinkle 1.8 s step-end so it flickers instead of fading. Eyes follow the pointer up to 2.5 units with 180 ms ease-out; a blink is an 80 ms squash every 3.5 to 6 s.
How it is built
One inline SVG. Hidden parts sit in the resting drawing at opacity 0 and a small scale or offset. Each part has its own transform origin (transform-box: fill-box) and its own delay, so the change reads as a sequence. Hover and focus-visible trigger the same state, so keyboard users get it too.
Where it fits
A nav mark, a 404 page, a loading mascot. Change the costume per season or per launch; keep the parts inside the mark's own silhouette and palette.
Reduced motion
The costume still appears, instantly, with no flicker.
Becomes slop when
The mark animates on its own at rest, the surprise is bigger than the nav, or it is a generic sparkle that any logo could have.
