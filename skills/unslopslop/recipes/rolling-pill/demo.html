Rolling status pill

A small pill above a headline that rolls between two or three short, true statements: what shipped, how often you update, what is live. Each line slides up out of the pill while the next rises in, and the pill width follows the new line, so nothing jumps.

Timing
Roll and resize: 420 ms, cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1), both at once. Hold: about 3 s. Pauses while hovered or focused and in hidden tabs.
How it is built
The pill clips its content. The next line is placed one line below and moves up as the current line moves up and fades. Before the roll, the next line is measured in a hidden sizer with the same font, and the pill width is set from the old value to the new one so the transition has two numbers to work between.
Content rule
Only statements you can prove at the moment the page is read. A live count ("27 signed up today") must come from real data or not be there; an invented number is the fastest way to lose trust. aria-live is polite, and the lines are short enough to read in the hold time.
Reduced motion
The first line stays and nothing rolls.
Becomes slop when
The pill says "New" or "Introducing" with nothing behind it, the numbers are made up, or it rolls through more than three lines.
