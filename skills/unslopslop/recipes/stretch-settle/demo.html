Stretch and settle

A selection indicator that moves in two phases. First it stretches so it covers both the old and the new choice, fast and without overshoot. Then it lets go of the old side and settles on the new one with a small overshoot. It reads as a soft object being pulled, where a plain slide reads as a box on rails.

Timing
Stretch: 190 ms, cubic-bezier(.32, .72, .24, 1). Settle: 420 ms, cubic-bezier(.28, 1.28, .36, 1) for position and (.24, 1.34, .38, 1) for width. Label colour 260 ms. Press: the whole control tilts 4 degrees toward the pressed side and scales to 0.99 for 160 ms, then springs back in 420 ms.
How it is built
The indicator is one absolutely placed element. On a click the script measures both buttons, sets the stretch phase (left edge at the smaller of the two, width spanning both), waits for that transition to end, then sets the settle phase on the target. A click during the stretch starts again from where the indicator is.
Where it fits
Segmented controls, tab bars, a filter row, a theme switch. Small elements only: on a full-width panel the overshoot looks like a mistake.
Reduced motion
The indicator jumps to the target. Selection is also carried by the label colour and aria-pressed, never by motion alone.
Becomes slop when
Every control on the page wobbles, the overshoot is large enough to be noticed on its own, or the same stretch is used for page transitions.
