All recipes

Letter window

One round letter of the wordmark becomes a window. Pictures flick through it fast, it lands on the hero picture, and the line under the mark arrives word by word with a small picture pill between two words. The mark itself never moves; only what is seen through it changes.

Measured beats
About ten pictures at 200 ms each (12 frames at 60 fps), hard cuts, no fades. Land on the hero picture and hold. Then words about 300 ms apart, each rising 8 px and fading in 300 ms; the picture pill pops from 0.6 to 1 in 360 ms with a small overshoot. Total about 4 s, then it rests on the final frame.
How it is built
render(t) is a pure function of time: the picture index is floor(t / flick) until the landing time, every word and the pill read their own progress from t. The slider scrubs it, Play drives it with requestAnimationFrame and stops at the end. That makes it testable, exportable as frames and safe for reduced motion.
Sizing the window
The circle is about 0.56 em of the mark's size, sitting on the baseline, so it occupies the letter's place without a ring. Check it against the real letter of the brand font; the counter has to read as the letter at a glance.
Where it fits
Brand film openers, launch heroes, social cut-downs (1080 x 1350 and 1080 x 1920). The pictures should be the brand's real use moments.
Reduced motion
Shows the final frame.
Becomes slop when
The window shows unrelated stock, the flick never stops, or two letters both become windows.
