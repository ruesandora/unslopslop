# Style: pantry bold

Status: **early**.

## Defining traits
Heavy, wide, soft-terminal display type (800 to 900) for headlines, quotes and the wordmark; small light body. A brand ink (deep chocolate, green or navy) replaces black for type and full-bleed bands. Warm cream ground, one saturated action colour. Devices with jobs: a rotating circular text badge carrying the brand promise, a marquee band carrying benefits or proof, a comparison table with "our" column filled in the action colour.

## When it fits
Food, pet, snacks, household direct-to-consumer stores, social campaigns for them.

## Combination (example)
cream ground ~60%, chocolate ink ~25%, yolk yellow action ~5% (as a fill behind ink text, never as text on cream), kraft and tan in photography ~10%.

## Motion
Slow badge rotation, constant marquee drift (both stop under reduced motion), arrow-driven carousels.

## Slop and bland versions
- Bland: storefront in white, grey and a stock blue button, product shots on white, no brand ink, no weight in the type.
- Slop: devices without content (a spinning badge that says nothing, a marquee of emoji).

## Signature shape (from measured reference C25)
A food brand can carry a whole system on one simple geometric shape drawn from the product (an egg seen from above: a disc of 1.00 D with an off-centre core of 0.44 D). Give it three jobs: recognition (flat shape), appetite (a real food photo inside the shape) and count (the pack's real quantity as a grid, for example 2 x 3). Two loud grounds (a violet and a pink at about 50/15) with a lime and a yolk as smaller fields work when every text pair is checked (white on violet about 8.9:1, ink on lime about 15:1, no small white text on pink).
