# Style: blueprint mono

Status: **early**.

## Defining traits
One saturated hue as the whole field, white for type and cards, and a lighter tint of the same hue (35 to 45%) for a 1 px grid and single-weight line drawings. Visible grid lines at section edges, crosshair corners on panels, isometric wireframe illustrations. No second hue.

## When it fits
Developer tools, infrastructure, research, "how it works" pages. Poor fit: food, beauty, warmth-led brands.

## Combination (example)
field electric blue ~70%, white type and cards ~22%, blue tint for grid and line art ~6%, near-black text inside white cards ~2%.

## Type
A neutral grotesk in light to regular weights; a two-line headline where the second line is the noun that changes.

## Motion
Line drawings draw in (stroke) and crossfade on hover, 300 to 500 ms; the headline noun swaps in step. A navigation preview panel can redraw one drawing per hovered item, so the menu becomes a product tour.

## Slop and bland versions
- Bland: the grid and line art removed, leaving a flat blue page (a monochrome page needs a drawn layer).
- Slop: glowing neon lines, a second accent hue, grid wallpaper over the whole page with no content in the cells.
