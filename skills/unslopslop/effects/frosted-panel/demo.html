Frosted panel

One pane of frosted glass over a real scene, in CSS only: the backdrop is blurred and a little more saturated, a lit top rim and a faint inner line give the edge, and a fine noise keeps the blur from looking like plastic.

Cost
backdrop-filter repaints the area under the pane whenever anything behind it moves. One pane is fine; a frosted sticky header over a scrolling page costs on every scroll frame on low-end phones. Keep the blur under about 24 px there.
Fallback
@supports gives a near-opaque tinted fill where backdrop-filter is missing, so the text stays readable.
Becomes slop when
Every card is frosted, it floats over a purple-to-blue blob, or the text on it drops under 4.5:1 against the brightest spot behind. The detector flags frosted glass as a default move: use it once, where the scene behind is the point.
