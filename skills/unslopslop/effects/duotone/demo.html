Duotone

Any photo in two colours. One SVG filter turns it to grey, then maps black to the shadow ink and white to the highlight ink. It ties mismatched stock or user photos to one palette, and it is pure CSS on an img.

Cost
Two filter primitives per image, run by the browser compositor. Cheap; fine on a grid of card images. No script needed in production: paste the SVG once and add filter: url(#duo).
Fallback
Where SVG filters on HTML fail (very old browsers) the photo simply shows in colour.
Becomes slop when
Pink and blue by default, or applied to product photos where the real colour is what people are buying. Pick the two inks from the project palette.
