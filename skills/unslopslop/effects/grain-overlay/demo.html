Grain overlay

Film grain with no canvas. An SVG turbulence filter is drawn once into a small tile, then laid over any section as a CSS background with a blend mode. It removes banding from CSS gradients and gives flat colour a material.

The whole effect, ready to paste:


    

      
Cost
Close to free. The tile is a few hundred bytes of SVG; the browser rasterises it once. The flicker is a transform on a composited layer (steps, 8 fps), not a repaint.

      
Fallback
Nothing to fall back from: every browser in use supports SVG filters in a data URI. Reduced motion: the flicker is off and the grain stays.

      
Becomes slop when
Opacity is high enough that text looks dirty (keep it under about 0.35 with overlay), it flickers on every section, or it sits on top of photos that are already grainy.
