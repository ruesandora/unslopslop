Card drift

A loose field of small image cards around a clear centre. Three depths: near cards are larger and sharp, far cards are small, paler and slightly blurred. They either fall and turn slowly, or fly outward from the middle and grow as they come closer. The centre stays empty for the title and the one action.

How it works
Each card has a depth (0.45, 0.7 or 1), a position, a turn and a turn speed. Size, speed, opacity and blur follow depth. Cards inside the clear ellipse fade out, so the field frames the centre instead of covering it. In "fly outward" a card is born near the centre at opacity 0, grows as it travels and is reborn when it leaves the frame. Only transform and opacity change per frame.
Measured starting values
Near cards about 100 x 66 to 170 x 108 px, turns within plus or minus 5 to 30 degrees, drift 20 to 30 px per second, pointer shift 2 to 8 px. A pale ground (about 95 percent lightness) keeps a busy field calm.
Cost
One composited layer per card. Up to about 60 cards is cheap; blur on far cards is a static filter, not animated. Pauses off screen and in hidden tabs.
Fallback and reduced motion
Reduced motion places the cards once and never moves them. Without script the centre content still shows on the plain ground.
Becomes slop when
The cards are random stock photos with no link to the product, the field also runs behind every later section, or cards cross the title. The cards should be the product's own content (saved items, generated images, real work).
