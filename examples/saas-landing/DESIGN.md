# Fluxly (example direction)

## Overview
Fluxly chases late invoices for freelancers. Tone: a calm, exact bookkeeper. Paper-and-ink look, one green accent, no effects.
Signature move: the reminder schedule is drawn as a real invoice timeline in the hero, not described.

## Colors
- paper `#f6f3ec`, ink `#1d1b17`, muted ink `#5a554b`, line `#d9d3c5`, surface `#fffdf8`
- accent (one): `#1f6f5c`, used for the primary button and links only

## Typography
- Headings: Charter, "Iowan Old Style", Georgia, serif; weight 500
- Body: system-ui stack; 17 px / 1.6
- Scale: 56 / 32 / 20 / 17 / 14

## Layout
- 4 px rhythm. Content column 1040 px; running text 62 ch. Left aligned.

## Elevation
- None. Separation by 1 px lines and surface colour.

## Shapes
- Radius 6 px for controls, 10 px for the one card.

## Components
- Primary button: accent fill, 12/20 padding. Secondary: text link.
- Invoice timeline: one card, table rows, status as text plus colour.

## Do / Don't
- Do say what happens on which day. Don't claim numbers we cannot source.
- Don't add gradients, glow, avatars, stat strips or numbered decoration.

## Motion
- Link and button colour change 120 ms ease-out. Nothing moves on load or scroll. Reduced motion: no transitions.
