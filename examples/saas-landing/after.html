The money lands, the chase stops

Fluxly reads your bank feed and matches incoming transfers to invoice numbers. A match cancels every reminder still waiting, so nobody is chased for money they already sent.

Before you connect anything

Can I edit a reminder before it goes out?

Yes. Every message waits in your queue for 24 hours. Change the text, move the date or cancel it there.

Does it write to my client on weekends?

No. Letters go out on weekdays between 9:00 and 17:00 in your client's time zone.

What if a client disputes the invoice?

Pause the schedule from the invoice page. Nothing else is sent until you resume it.

Can Fluxly move money from my account?

No. It reads your bank feed with read-only access, only to match incoming payments to invoice numbers. Disconnect the bank in Settings at any time and Fluxly stops reading at once.
