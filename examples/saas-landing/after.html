It stops the second you are paid

Fluxly reads your bank feed and matches incoming transfers to invoice numbers. A match cancels every reminder still waiting, so nobody gets chased for money they already sent.

Questions freelancers ask

Can I edit a reminder before it goes out?

Yes. Every message waits in the queue for 24 hours. Change the text, move the date or cancel it there.

Does it write to my client on weekends?

No. Letters go out on weekdays between 9:00 and 17:00 in your client's time zone.

What if a client disputes the invoice?

Pause the schedule from the invoice page. Nothing else is sent until you resume it.
