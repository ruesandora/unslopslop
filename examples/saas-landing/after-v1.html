What happens after you send an invoice

  1. Fluxly watches the due date

    It reads the invoice from your accounting tool and starts a schedule the day it becomes overdue.

  2. It writes in your voice

    You approve the three templates once.

  3. It stops when paid

    Bank transactions are matched to invoice numbers.

Questions freelancers ask

Can I edit a reminder before it goes out?

Yes. Every message waits in a queue for 24 hours, and you can change or cancel it there.

Does it contact my client on weekends?

No. Reminders are sent on weekdays between 9:00 and 17:00 in your client's time zone.

What if a client disputes the invoice?

Pause the schedule from the invoice page. Nothing else is sent until you resume it.
